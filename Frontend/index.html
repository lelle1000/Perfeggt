Welcome to Perfeggt! A quick and efficient egg boiling app for someone who has no time to waste!

Step 1: Boil up your water

To achieve consistent results, bring the water to a boil before adding the eggs. This ensures they always start cooking under the same conditions.

Step 2: Warm up your eggs

If the eggs have been refrigerated, they’re more likely to crack when placed directly into boiling water. To prevent this, run them under warm tap water first to let them warm up slightly before cooking.

Step 3: Use a spoon

Putting an egg directly into boiling water can cause it to crack, so place it on a spoon before carefully lowering it in.

Step 4: Lower the tempature

Lastly, once all eggs have been gently lowered into the pot, start the timer, reduce the heat to a gentle simmer, and wait!
